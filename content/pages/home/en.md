## Research Domain: Geometric Data Analysis

Brigitte Le Roux's work focuses on *Geometric Data Analysis* (GDA) and its applications. The methods studied include in particular:

- **Multiple Correspondence Analysis** (MCA)
- **Principal Component Analysis** (PCA)
- **Structured Data Analysis**
- **Combinatorial Inference**
